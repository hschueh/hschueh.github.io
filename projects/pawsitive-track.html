Pawsitive Track

A dog-walk tracker for iOS and Android that records the route and what each dog did along the way.

  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Zustand
  • Google Maps

Product page →

What it does

  • Records the walk on a live map, including in the background.
  • Logs pee, poop, water, photos and notes per dog, pinned to the map.
  • Remembers spots you mark, like a barking dog behind a gate, and shows them on later walks.
  • Shows every walk on one heatmap, with badges for streaks and distance.

How it's built

No backend and no database. Each walk is saved as a standard GPX file, with dog events stored as waypoints, and the history is read back from those files. Any GPX tool can open a walk, and a backup is just the files.
