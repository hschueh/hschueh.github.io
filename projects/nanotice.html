Nanotice

An Android notification inbox that summarizes, triages, and acts on your notifications with an on-device LLM — nothing is sent to the cloud.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Gemini Nano
  • LiteRT-LM
  • Room
  • Koin

What it is

Nanotice listens to incoming notifications, groups them by app and conversation, and turns each group into a short card: a summary, an urgency level, and a suggested one-tap action — reply, open a link, call, add to calendar. Cards can be archived toward inbox zero.

An assistant tab runs a small local agent over the same data: it can list notifications, act on them, archive them, and call tools exposed by other installed apps. Every action stops at a confirm dialog first, since notification text is untrusted input.

How it's built

Inference runs on the phone through two runtimes behind one interface: Gemini Nano via ML Kit GenAI, and open models (e.g. Qwen, Gemma) via LiteRT-LM. A compare mode shows the engines side by side. The agent manages its own context window — compacting old turns so a 2B-class model can hold a long conversation — and discovers callable tools by parsing other apps' shortcuts.

It's a sideloaded prototype for exploring what small on-device models can do, not a published app.
