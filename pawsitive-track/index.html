Every walk tells a tail.

A walk tracker for dog people. It follows the dogs, not just the distance, so you always know who peed, where, and which gate to steer clear of tomorrow.

In development for iOS and Android

Fitness apps treat a dog walk like a jog: distance, pace, done. But on the way home, the things you're actually wondering are:

  • Did both of them pee?
  • When did Mochi last poop?
  • Which house had the dog that lunges at the fence?

Pawsitive Track answers those. The route is just the map everything else gets pinned to.

Log what each dog does, without breaking stride

Tap a dog, tap what happened: pee, poop, water, a photo, a note. Every tap gives a little buzz and a +1, so you can log one-handed with the leash in the other hand and barely look down.

Start near a familiar spot and the app picks the same dogs you walked there last time.

Remember the spots that matter

Leave a note on the map, like “😠 dog behind this gate”, “🗑️ trash can here” or “🐿️ squirrel tree”, and it shows up again on every walk that passes by.

See it again and log it again, and the spot counts the sighting instead of adding a duplicate pin.

Hand the leash to a sitter

Every dog gets a profile: their walk stats, photos from past walks, and the notes you'd otherwise text to a sitter at the last minute, such as feeding, quirks and who to call.

And the things a tracker should just do

  • 📱
    Keeps going in your pocket.

    Tracking carries on with the screen off. On Android, the walk can sit on your lock screen.

  • 🛟
    Survives a crash.

    If the app quits mid-walk, it offers to pick up where you left off.

  • ❤️
    Syncs to your health app.

    Walks land in Apple Health or Health Connect automatically.

  • 🖼️
    Shares nicely.

    Turn a walk photo into a postcard with the route and stats on top, in any size from a story to a widescreen.

  • 💾
    Your data, your files.

    Walks export as standard GPX. A full backup moves everything to a new phone.

  • 🌗
    Light, dark, 中文.

    Follows your system theme, in English or Traditional Chinese.
