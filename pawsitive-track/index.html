Pawsitive Track How it's built

Every walk tells a tail.

A walk tracker for dog people. It follows the dogs, not just the distance.

  • 🐕

    Log each dog

    Pee, poop, water and photos, tapped per dog and pinned where they happened.

  • 📍

    Remember spots

    Mark the barking dog behind the gate once. It shows up on every walk after.

  • 🔥

    Look back

    Every walk on one heatmap, with streaks and badges along the way.
