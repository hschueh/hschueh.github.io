我假裝醉倒在桌上，偷看掌櫃把信藏在哪。

17 察覺 d20 + 3 = 17，對 DC 14 成功

掌櫃左右張望，把信塞進櫃台後那只缺了口的酒罈底下。你打了個響亮的呼嚕，他才放心地走開。

三天後

我再回到客棧，點一壺酒。

掌櫃端酒過來，瞇眼打量你：「客官，那晚醉得可不輕啊。」他的目光往那只酒罈飄了一下。

  • Type anything

    No command list. Say what you want to do in your own words, and the dice decide whether it works.

  • A world that remembers

    People, places and promises carry over from one session to the next. Lie to an innkeeper and they'll remember the next time you walk in.

  • Any world you like

    Wuxia, cyberpunk Taipei, a school of magic. New places and characters appear as you explore.
